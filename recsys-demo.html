Technology

Built on best-in-class APIs

Every component was chosen for latency. The full voice round-trip — from guest utterance to spoken reply — completes in under 3 seconds.

📞

Plivo

Bidirectional audio stream over WebSocket. Mulaw 8kHz, real-time barge-in support.

🎙️

Deepgram Nova-2

Streaming speech-to-text. Interim results enable barge-in; 600ms silence endpointing.

🧠

Claude Haiku (Anthropic)

Handles open-ended room service questions. ~1.5s latency, responses cached by hash.

🔊

ElevenLabs Turbo v2

Luxury hotel voice. MP3 → mulaw via ffmpeg subprocess. 640-byte chunks paced at 75ms.

⚡

FastAPI + asyncio

Single async event loop handles WebSocket, Deepgram reader, and TTS task concurrently.

🗄️

MySQL + SQLAlchemy

Stores call logs, menu text, and restaurant config. Every conversation is auditable.
